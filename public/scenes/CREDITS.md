# Scene pictures

From couch-tv (https://github.com/Seagey/couch-tv, tv-home/web/backgrounds), MIT licence (Copyright (c) 2026 Seagey).
The scenes were made for couch-tv with an AI image model from text only; couch-tv's CREDITS.md says "Use them however
you like". `-fg.webp` is the scenery cut out of each scene (drawn over the clock), `-glass.jpg` a blurred, brightened
copy used as the clock's frosted fill, `@2x` the 4K copies. The clock font is Antonio (SIL Open Font License), also as
shipped by couch-tv.
